About Me

Doctoral Researcher at the Czech Academy of Sciences and member of the Ornithology Department at the Natural History Museum of Lima. My research focuses on population genetics, ornithology, ecology, and conservation biology. I have conducted fieldwork in Peru, Australia, and Norway. Fluent in 9 languages including Spanish, English, French, Portuguese, German, and Czech.
